MyMu: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

MyMu is a native iOS client for Claude Code UI (the open-source claudecodeui server). MyMu connects to a server that you configure in the app; it ships with a public demo server so you can try it before pointing it at your own. This policy describes how the MyMu app handles your data.

What the app collects

Nothing is sent to the app developer. MyMu has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Data you provide, and where it goes

  • Server address, username and password: sent only to the server you configure, to sign you in. Your password is never stored on the device; only the returned session token is kept.
  • Session token: stored securely in the iOS Keychain and sent to your server to authenticate requests.
  • Conversation content and files: transmitted to and from your configured server so you can view and drive your Claude Code sessions.

All of this data is exchanged directly between the app and the server you choose. How that data is stored and processed on the server side is governed by the operator of that server (you or your organization).

Data stored on your device

MyMu stores, locally on your device only: the server address, your recently viewed conversations, and cached account display info (in app preferences), and the session token (in the Keychain). You can remove all of it by signing out or deleting the app.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. The app developer never receives it.
